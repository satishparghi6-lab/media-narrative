SOURCE REGISTER: Traditional and Digital Media Framing of Youth Protest Narratives in India

PURPOSE
This dataset accompanies the manuscript "Traditional and Digital Media Framing of Youth Protest Narratives in India". It lists the 15 publicly available media accounts of the June-July 2026 Indian youth protests that were analysed in the study, so that readers can locate the original material. It supports research transparency only. It does not reproduce any third-party articles, videos or posts, which remain hosted by their publishers and platforms and are subject to their access conditions and copyright.

FILES
- Youth_Protest_Media_Source_Register.csv : the 15-source register
- README.txt : this file

COLUMNS IN THE CSV
Source_ID           Number 1-15, matching the numbered source register in the manuscript
Outlet_or_Channel   Publisher, broadcaster or channel
Title_as_cited      Title of the item as cited in the manuscript
Source_Type         Kind of item (news analysis, fact-check, television clip, etc.)
Category_in_Study   One of three groups used in the study: Analysis of the coverage (5);
                    Report on 21 July Parliament march (5); Report on 20 July Chalo Sansad march (5)
Platform            Web, YouTube or Instagram
URL                 Link to the item
Manuscript_Citation In-text citation key used in the manuscript
Date_Accessed       Date the item was last accessed (to be completed by the authors)
Verification_Note   Items needing a check before publication

SOURCE SELECTION
Fifteen accounts from June and July 2026 were selected to represent the range of coverage across print, television, independent digital and nationalist sources. Each was analysed through close reading and thematic coding (see the Methodology section of the manuscript).

RELATION TO THE MANUSCRIPT
The register corresponds to the 15 media accounts listed in the manuscript's Data Availability Statement. No participant-level or experimental data were generated.
